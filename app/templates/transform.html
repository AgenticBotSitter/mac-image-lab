WHAT TO EXPECT

Best effort, not identity lock

Qwen can use the source image to carry forward subjects, composition, and visual context while making a requested change. It may still reinterpret faces, hands, clothing, objects, or composition.

For a stronger comparison, change one thing at a time: background, visual treatment, wardrobe, or era.

Do not use this as a guarantee of exact personal likeness. Review every result before keeping or sharing it.
