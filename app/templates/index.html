Qwen-Image-2.1 prompt guide — read before generating

Best rule: describe the finished image, not a bag of keywords. State the medium, subject, framing, background, materials, light, palette, and composition. Set aspect ratio and resolution with the UI controls—not with 4K, 8K, or 16:9 in the prompt.

Use this order

  1. Medium and style: photograph, illustration, oil painting, 3D render, poster.
  2. Subject: the specific thing/person and important observable attributes.
  3. Framing and placement: close-up, wide, centred, lower-right, negative space.
  4. Scene and background: describe what surrounds the subject.
  5. Materials and light: texture, finish, source, direction, highlight, shadow.
  6. Composition and mood: balanced, sparse, warm, cinematic, editorial.

What Qwen 2.1 is good at

  • Detailed materials, lighting, composition, portrait/product fidelity, and refined visual texture.
  • Visible text when you supply the exact words in straight double quotes and say where/how it appears.
  • Native transparent RGBA assets: This is an RGBA image with transparency. [description]. The image has alpha channel and the background is transparent.
  • Image editing and up to ten references—planned here only after the Apple-MPS edit workflow is proven.

Avoid

  • Vague prompts such as pretty flowers, masterpiece, 8K; they leave the model to invent the frame.
  • Conflicting styles, lighting, camera angles, or long uncounted lists of objects.
  • Dense or tiny text; quote every required visible word and inspect the output.
  • Assuming Fast is final quality. Use it for composition; use Standard or Maximum Native intentionally for the selected concept.

Quick template

A [orientation] [medium/style] of [subject], [placement/framing], against [specific background]. [Materials and important details]. [Direction/quality] lighting creates [highlights/shadows]. The composition is [mood/palette/layout]. No readable text, logo, signature, or watermark.
