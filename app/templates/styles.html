QWEN-IMAGE-2.1 · STYLE RECIPES

Style guides

Practical prompt structures grounded in Qwen’s official advice: describe the finished frame, choose the medium, specify material and lighting, and place important elements clearly.

Photorealistic nature

Use for: landscapes, wildlife, travel scenes.

A wide realistic landscape photograph of [subject] in [specific location], foreground [details], midground [details], distant [details]. Natural [time-of-day] light enters from [direction], with [weather/atmosphere]. The palette is [colors] and the composition uses [negative space / leading line / balanced horizon].

Tip: Name foreground, midground, and background so the scene has depth.

Editorial product photograph

Use for: objects, tools, packaging, tabletop studies.

A square editorial product photograph of [object], centered on [surface], against [specific background]. The object is [material, color, finish] with [physical details]. Soft [direction] studio light creates [shadow and highlight behavior]. Leave [location] as clean negative space. No readable text, logo, or watermark.

Tip: Describe surfaces, not just the object name: brushed metal, matte ceramic, frosted glass, woven linen.

Painterly botanical still life

Use for: florals, vintage interiors, textured art studies.

A vertical painterly still life of [flowers/objects] arranged in [vessel] on [surface]. The background is [specific wall or cloth] in [palette]. [Direction] window light creates [highlights and shadows]. Visible [canvas grain / brushwork / paint texture] gives the image a tactile finish. The composition is calm and [balanced/asymmetrical].

Tip: Give the vase, table, wall, and light equal specificity—do not leave the background blank.

Hand-painted fantasy animation

Use for: warm storybook scenes without requesting a named studio’s exact look.

A hand-painted fantasy animation scene of [subject] in [place], with soft watercolor-like backgrounds, expressive shapes, gentle atmospheric perspective, and warm natural color. [Lighting]. The scene feels [quiet / adventurous / nostalgic] and uses a clear readable composition.

Tip: Describe visual traits directly rather than relying on a studio name alone.

Portrait editorial

Use for: fashion and character studies.

A vertical editorial portrait photograph of an adult [subject], [pose] in [setting], wearing [garment material and color]. [Direction and quality] light shapes the face, hair, and clothing. The background falls into [depth-of-field/texture]. The composition is [mood] with [palette].

Tip: State pose, gaze, garment, lighting, and setting. Do not depend on a single adjective such as “beautiful.”

Transparent asset

Use for: stickers, isolated objects, compositing elements.

This is an RGBA image with transparency. A [specific object or character], [pose/material/color/detail]. The image has alpha channel and the background is transparent.

Official Qwen wording: this exact structure is recommended for transparency.

Universal rules

  • Choose the aspect ratio and resolution in the app, not with vague prompt labels like 4K or 8K.
  • Use a Quality Profile first. Only open Advanced controls when you intentionally want a custom canvas, step count, or repeatable seed.
  • For visible image text, supply the exact intended words in straight double quotes and describe where they belong.
  • One coherent scene is stronger than conflicting styles, lighting conditions, and camera angles in one prompt.

Source basis: Qwen-Image-2.1 official README and official prompt-rewriter specification. These are practical recipes, not guarantees; compare and keep the best result.
